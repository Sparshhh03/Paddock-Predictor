How it works

A four-stage pipeline that reruns after every qualifying session and race.

1

Collect

Race and qualifying results for every Grand Prix since 2018, pulled from the Jolpica F1 database.

2

Engineer

20 signals per driver: grid slot, gap to pole, recent form, team pace, retirement rate, track history and championship standing.

3

Train

A LightGBM ranking model sets the order. Logistic models give win and podium odds. Recent seasons weigh more.

4

Test honestly

Every past race is predicted using only earlier races, then scored against the grid-order baseline.

Read the odds, not just the order. Crashes, safety cars, rain and strategy make F1 noisy. A 30% favourite loses most of the time. Before qualifying, the grid is estimated from team pace, so predictions sharpen after Saturday.
